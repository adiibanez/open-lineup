## The event and the organisers

- **Campeonato de Surf Junior y Open Almáciga 2026**: 30 October – 1 November
  2026, Playa de Almáciga (Parque Rural de Anaga, Santa Cruz de Tenerife), with
  Playa de El Roque as backup if the sea is too big. Valid for the Circuito
  Nacional Junior (Federación Española de Surfing) and the Circuito Canario Open
  and Junior (Federación Canaria de Surfing). Organised by the Servicio de
  Deportes of the Ayuntamiento de Santa Cruz de Tenerife and PerfectTen Surf
  School. Sponsors listed: Ayuntamiento de Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Ideco,
  MojoWax, Libby's, Surpan, Lafast, Moly. Champions receive a €200 scholarship
  and a direct pass to the 2027 Spanish Junior championship; Open categories
  share €250 each.
  Sources: fcsurf.es event page (published 16 Sep 2026); Deporpress,
  "Almáciga reúne a las promesas del surf nacional y canario este fin de
  semana".
- **2025 edition** (3–5 October 2025) had the same organisers; sponsors then
  were Mojowax, the Ayuntamiento, Fuente Alta, Lafast and Moly.
  Source: fcsurf.es, "Campeonato Almáciga Surfing".
- **The beach the user linked** resolves to **Playa de Benijo**, the next cove
  along from Almáciga in Anaga. Source: Google Maps link resolution.

## PerfectTen Surf School

- More than 500 students a year in lessons and camps. Group lessons: up to 8
  students per instructor, 1.5 h in the water, from €35; private lessons up to
  4 people. Beaches: Almáciga, Roque de las Bodegas, El Porís. Supported by
  Decathlon, Fuente Alta, Corona and the Ayuntamiento de Santa Cruz de
  Tenerife; the site says it promotes local surf and inclusive sport. Site is in
  Spanish, German and English.
  Source: perfectten.es (home and lessons pages).

## Parasurfing in the Canary Islands

- The **Campeonato de España de Parasurfing 2026** ran 12–14 June 2026 at La
  Cícer, Playa de Las Canteras, Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, as "Gran Canaria
  Surf No Limit Fundación DISA", inside the Gran Canaria Surf Fest, organised by
  the Spanish and Canarian federations with MPG Events and the Cabildo de Gran
  Canaria. Sources: avaibooksports.com registration page; RTVC (April 2026);
  hellocanaryislands.com.
- Regional tourism copy calls the islands "home to the best adapted surfing".
  Source: hellocanaryislands.com event listing.

## ALS in Spain

- 4,000–4,500 people living with ALS at any time; about 900 new diagnoses a
  year and a similar number of deaths, so prevalence stays flat. Source:
  Sociedad Española de Neurología figures as reported by The Objective (Oct
  2025) and others; Fundación Luzón Observatorio report (2024).
- The **Ley ELA** was approved in November 2024; a November 2025 royal
  decree‑law created a new dependency grade, Grado III+, for people needing
  round‑the‑clock care. Source: La Moncloa, "Ley ELA: medidas y beneficios".
- Why it matters here: ALS leaves cognition intact and most people communicate
  through eye‑gaze devices they already own, which is exactly the input the
  home app targets.

## Starlink Mini

- Dish about 1.1 kg (29–30 × 25–26 × 3.7 cm), IP67, typical draw 25–40 W (peaks
  higher at startup), built‑in Wi‑Fi, DC input. Typical speeds reported in 2026
  reviews: 100–200+ Mbps down, 15–25 Mbps up. US hardware price has moved
  between about $199 and $499; Roam plans about $50–175/month depending on data
  tier and country. Sources: Starlink published specs as relayed by
  rvmobileinternet.com; satelliteinternet.com review (June 2026);
  macmyths.com (Aug 2026). Exact pricing and plan availability in Spain to be
  confirmed on starlink.com.

## Moonlight (the lights)

- **Moonlight Norway Mountain Gear AS**, Strandgata 10, 6300 Åndalsnes, Norway.
  Makes "Bright As Day" headlamps with aluminium heads, active thermal design
  that holds real lumen output across the battery, GoPro quick‑clip mounts,
  21700‑cell batteries. Strongest head: **Bright As Day 4000** — 4,000 lm,
  IP67, 522 g with battery, 1.5–30 h, listed at NOK 4,999. Bright As Day 3000:
  3,000 lm, 500 g, NOK 3,699. 71 Wh (14.8 V, 4,800 mAh) battery sold
  separately at NOK 2,199. The site has a "Proffbruker" (professional user)
  form and a "Relight" repair/refurbish programme.
  Source: no.moonlightmountaingear.com product and collection pages.
- These are headlamps, not floodlights. The proposed rig uses two or three
  heads on a pan‑tilt bracket via their GoPro mount; see `night-light-rig.md`.
