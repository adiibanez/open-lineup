Why everything stays open

Open source, open hardware

Firmware, apps, bracket drawings and the build guide are published under permissive licences. A school in Galicia or Portugal can rebuild the station without asking us.

Participants own their data

Heart rate, voice and video from a person with ALS are theirs. Nothing is sold, nothing trains a model, and every recording can be deleted by the person it shows.

Non‑profit by design

Partner money buys kit and beach days, not equity. Sponsors get the story and the footage; the kit itself becomes a loan library for events and schools.

Assistive technology works best as a commons. Too many assistive devices die with their vendors; this one cannot, because nobody owns it.
