After sunset

A light that follows your student

The mast carries two or three battery floodlight heads on a motorised pan‑tilt. The station already tracks the active rider for the camera; the lights follow the same bearing, so one instructor and one student get a lit lane on an otherwise dark bay.

Candidate heads: Moonlight (Åndalsnes, Norway) Bright As Day 4000 — 4,000 lm, IP67, 522 g with battery, 1.5–30 h, GoPro quick‑clip mount. Three heads ≈ a car’s high beams, pointed where it matters.

Honest framing: this assists a short night session for experienced surfers, with lifeguards and the park’s permission. It does not, on its own, make night surfing safe.
