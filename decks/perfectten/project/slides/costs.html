What it costs you, and what it gives you

It costs you

  • No money. We bring the station, the cameras and the lights, and we run them.
  • One instructor who is willing to wear a camera and talk a little more than usual.
  • Two test mornings before anyone pays for anything.
  • Honest feedback when it gets in the way of a lesson, so we fix it.

It gives you

  • The first surf school in Spain with a remote‑student programme, and the story that comes with it.
  • A paid family‑link extra that your camps can sell from day one.
  • Something concrete behind “inclusive sport” for Decathlon, the Ayuntamiento and future partners.
  • Replays of every lesson for your coaching and your channels, free.
  • Kit and software that stay open source; nobody can take them away or charge you later.
