With Dani (instructor) · group of 6

1

Warm‑up and theory on the sand: the remote student is on the call, sees the whiteboard and the group, and is introduced like anyone else.

2

In the water they ride along with the instructor’s camera, or with the volunteer who is “their body” that day, and look around freely.

3

They can speak to the instructor between sets: one held gaze opens the mic. “Wait for the second one.” “Paddle now.”

4

Feedback round as usual. The remote student gets the replay of “their” best wave to keep.
