The station fits in one car boot

Two people set it up in about thirty minutes. No generator, no cable across the sand, nothing fixed to the beach; it leaves with us when the heat is over.

Starlink Mini

About 1.1 kg, 25–40 W, IP67. Typically 100+ Mbps down and 15–25 up on a Roam plan: enough for two live 360° feeds.

Beach radio

A directional Wi‑Fi link aimed at the lineup, plus a mesh node by the judges. Phones and cameras in the water talk to this, not to the satellite.

Edge box

A fanless mini computer: mixes the feeds, buffers them when a surfer duck‑dives, runs the pairing and the vote. Open source, ours.

Cameras

A 360° camera on the mast for the whole bay, a zoom camera that follows the active surfer, and the surfer’s own board or wrist camera.

Power

One 1 kWh lithium battery runs the dish, radio and computer for a full day of heats. A folding panel tops it up; the lights get their own packs.
