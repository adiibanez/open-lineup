1

Before the heat, each surfer is paired with a co‑rider at home. They meet on the call, hear the briefing, see the lineup.

2

During the ride the co‑rider sees the board’s 360° view, looks around with eye‑gaze or head movement, and hears the water.

3

Between waves they talk: one held gaze opens the mic to the surfer’s waterproof phone. Short, real, both ways.

4

Co‑riders vote a people’s wave of the heat. The official judging stays exactly as it is.
