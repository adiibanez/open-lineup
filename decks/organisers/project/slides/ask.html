What we ask, and what you get

We ask for

  • One expression heat, or one open session, in the Almáciga 2027 programme
  • Two or three surfers willing to carry a camera and wear a phone
  • Your help with the permit for a temporary station inside the Parque Rural de Anaga
  • A corner of the beach near the judges’ tent and a line on the poster
  • Access to one beach test day before the event, ideally in spring

You get

  • The first national‑circuit event with a remote‑participation heat, with your name on it
  • Free live 360° coverage of the whole weekend for your channels
  • A story your sponsors and the Ayuntamiento’s inclusion agenda can carry
  • A kit and software that stay open source and free for any FCS or FES event afterwards
  • A direct bridge to the parasurfing community already competing in the islands

We bring the hardware and the people, and arrange cover for the station [insurer to confirm]. Nothing in the official competition changes.
