Almáciga already runs the hard part

This weekend the beach hosts the Campeonato de Surf Junior y Open Almáciga 2026: heats, judges, permits, a programme, a crowd. Everything a competition needs is here.

The one thing missing is a way for people who cannot get to the sand, or into the water, to be in the lineup too. That is the part we build.

The Canary Islands already call themselves home to Spain’s adapted surfing: the national parasurfing championship ran at La Cícer in June. Almáciga is the natural next step.

Almáciga 2026 at a glance

Dates30 Oct – 1 Nov 2026, Playa de Almáciga (El Roque as backup)
CircuitsCircuito Nacional Junior (FES) and Circuito Canario Open + Junior (FCS)
OrganisersServicio de Deportes, Ayuntamiento de Santa Cruz de Tenerife, with PerfectTen Surf School
PartnersIdeco, MojoWax, Libby’s, Surpan, Lafast, Moly
Incentives€200 scholarship per champion; €250 per Open category; direct pass to the 2027 Spanish Junior championship

Sources: fcsurf.es event page (16 Sep 2026); Deporpress; RTVC on the Gran Canaria Surf Fest 2026
