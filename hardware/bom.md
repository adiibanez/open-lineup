## Power budget (rough)

| Load | W | Hours | Wh |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starlink Mini | 35 | 8 | 280 |
| Edge box | 25 | 8 | 200 |
| Radio + mesh | 15 | 8 | 120 |
| Cameras + pan‑tilt | 20 | 8 | 160 |
| **Total** | | | **~760 Wh** |

A 1 kWh pack covers a full day of heats with margin; the panel buys the second
day. Lamps run on their own packs (71 Wh each, ~1.5 h at full output per pack,
so one spare each covers a long night session).

## Not in the box

Permits, insurance, two crew, two volunteers, travel. See the funder deck's
tiers.
