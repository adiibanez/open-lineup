# Identity

## The mark

Two dots and an arc. One dot is warm and sits on black sand: the lamp on the
tripod, the station, whoever is on the beach. The other is pale and sits far
out past the wave: the rider in the lineup. The dotted arc between them is the
link, drawn as a trail of small steps rather than a solid line, because that is
what a radio link over water actually is. The wave is teal and runs under
everything.

Read it as a sentence: *someone on the beach, someone in the water, and a line
between them that holds.*

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Night / depth | `#0E1B2A` | backgrounds, type on light |
| Black sand | `#1A1A1E` | ground |
| Toned white | `#F4F1EA` | light backgrounds, type on dark |
| Lamp | `#E89B2C` | the one accent; the light, the arc, eyebrows |
| Water | `#2A9D8F` | the wave, links, secondary accent |

Never pure white or pure black. The amber is the only warm colour and it is
reserved for the thing that follows a person: the light, the arc, the vote.

## Type

Headlines in **Oswald** (condensed, poster, the way surf comps print heat
sheets). Body in **Public Sans** (civic, quiet, legible on a phone on a beach).

## Voice

Plain. No "revolutionary", no "empower", no exclamation marks. Say what the
kit does, what it costs, what it cannot do yet. Honest framing beats a bigger
claim every time, especially around night surfing and around people whose
time is short.
